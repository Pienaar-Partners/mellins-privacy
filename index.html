Privacy Policy

Mellins Customisation app · Last updated 13 November 2026

The Mellins Customisation app (the “App”) is used in Mellins optometry stores in South Africa to help customers explore eyewear and lens options and learn about smart glasses. It is developed by Pienaar Partners for Mellins (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where it applies, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Information the App uses

  • Vision profile answers: answers you give in store about how you use your eyes and what you want from your eyewear (for example screen time, driving, hobbies and style preferences). These are used to suggest suitable lenses and frames.
  • Face data: if you choose to use the frame recommendation feature, a photo of your face and measurements of your facial geometry, captured with the iPad’s front camera. This is explained in full in the Face Data section below.
  • Smart glasses assistant questions: questions you type into the built-in smart glasses assistant, such as how to pair glasses or which phones are compatible.
  • Diagnostic data: anonymous crash reports and performance data that Apple may share with us if analytics sharing is switched on in the device settings. These never include face data.

The App does not ask for your payment details, location or contacts, does not access the device’s photo library, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It uses the camera only for the frame recommendation feature. Please don’t type personal details such as your name, contact details or medical information into the smart glasses assistant.

Face Data

What we collect

When you choose to use the frame recommendation feature, the App uses the iPad’s front camera to capture a photo of your face and measurements of your facial geometry (such as the width and proportions of your face). We do not use face data to identify who you are.

How we use it

Face data is used only to determine your face shape so the App can recommend eyewear frames that suit you. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, identification, authentication or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

Where it is processed and stored

Face data is processed entirely on the in-store iPad, within the App. It is never sent to our servers, to Mellins systems, to Anthropic or to any other third party. It is held only in the App’s temporary memory while the feature is in use and is not saved to the device’s storage or photo library, iCloud, or any backup.

Retention and deletion

Face data is deleted automatically as soon as the frame recommendation has been shown and you leave the feature or your session ends. We do not keep face data after your visit, so there is no stored face data to delete.

Sharing

Face data is not shared with anyone. If this ever changes, we will update this policy before any face data is shared, and we will ensure that any third party that receives face data provides the same or equal protection of that data as described in this policy.

Your consent and control

The frame recommendation feature is optional. Face data is only captured if you agree to use it, and Mellins staff can help you choose frames without it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking staff to stop or by closing the feature, and any face data captured up to that point is deleted immediately.

How we use your information

  • To give you eyewear and lens recommendations during your visit.
  • To recommend frames that suit your face shape, if you use the frame recommendation feature.
  • To answer your questions about smart glasses.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The smart glasses assistant is powered by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic. When you ask a question, the text of your question is sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models. See Anthropic’s privacy policy. Face data and vision profile answers are never sent to Anthropic.

We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Vision profile answers and face data are used only during your session on the in-store iPad and are not kept by the App afterwards. Assistant questions are not stored by us beyond what is needed to provide the answer.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we process it, by emailing the address below. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Children

The App is used with the help of Mellins staff. Children should use it with a parent or guardian present, and the frame recommendation feature is only used for a child with their parent’s or guardian’s consent.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
