Privacy Policy

Mellins Customisation app · Last updated 9 October 2026

The Mellins Customisation app (the "App") is used in Mellins optometry stores in South Africa to help customers explore eyewear and lens options and learn about smart glasses. It is developed by Pienaar Partners for Mellins ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where it applies, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Information the App uses

  • Vision profile answers: answers you give in store about how you use your eyes and what you want from your eyewear (for example screen time, driving, hobbies and style preferences). These are used to suggest suitable lenses and frames.
  • Smart glasses assistant questions: questions you type into the built-in smart glasses assistant, such as how to pair glasses or which phones are compatible.
  • Diagnostic data: anonymous crash reports and performance data that Apple may share with us if analytics sharing is switched on in the device settings.

The App does not ask for your ID number, payment details, location, contacts or photos, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. Please don't type personal details such as your name, contact details or medical information into the smart glasses assistant.

How we use it

  • To give you eyewear and lens recommendations during your visit.
  • To answer your questions about smart glasses.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The smart glasses assistant is powered by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic. When you ask a question, the text of your question is sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Vision profile answers are used during your session on the in-store iPad and are not kept by the App afterwards. Assistant questions are not stored by us beyond what is needed to provide the answer.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we process it, by emailing the address below. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Children

The App is used with the help of Mellins staff. Children should use it with a parent or guardian present.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
